Nsight Compute 2026.2.1 connected to fraud_daemon_gpu_benchmark.exe but did not
collect hardware-counter sections. The installed NVIDIA driver returned:

  ERR_NVGPUCTRPERM - The user does not have permission to access NVIDIA GPU
  Performance Counters on the target device 0.

No attempt was made to bypass the host's profiler permission policy. Nsight
Systems CUDA trace data is preserved separately. Occupancy, Tensor Core
utilization, DRAM/L2 counters, register pressure, and warp-stall metrics remain
unmeasured and are not claimed in the paper.
